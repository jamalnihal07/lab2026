Additional Safety Features

  • Driver Verification: All drivers background-checked and verified
  • Ride Tracking: Family can track your live location during ride
  • Video Recording: In-cab camera records ride for evidence if needed
  • Driver Rating: See driver's safety rating before accepting ride
  • Women Safety: Special features for women passengers (night rides)
  • Ride Sharing Disabled: Ensure privacy and sole occupancy
  • Panic Button: Physical panic button in vehicle
  • Trip Details: Ride details emailed to emergency contacts
  • Emergency Contacts: Add/manage up to 5 emergency contacts
  • Incident Report: File incident report after emergency

Set Up Emergency Contacts

Step 1: Open Settings

Go to Profile → Safety Settings → Emergency Contacts

Step 2: Add Contacts

Add up to 5 trusted contacts (family, friends, workplace)

Step 3: Verify Numbers

Confirm contact phone numbers for alert delivery

Step 4: Share Live Location

Contacts can track your ride live in real-time
